AN OPEN-WORLD SURVIVAL RPG

COVEN

Once there were four witches, and the isle was theirs. Then the Unwitch rose at the Crown, the coven broke, and every sister fled to her own corner of the wild. Now you are back. Brew potions in a bubbling cauldron, ride your broom over misty hills, charm human merchants in a borrowed face, and win back three sisters. Only four witches casting as one can end her.

Your goal: gather the full coven of four, summon the Wyrd Titan at the Crown and cast the Grand Rite to unmake the Unwitch. Follow the crimson beacon.

Four lands, four witch paths, 40 creatures, one very bad witchHerbs, alchemy, a village to raise and a broom that learns to flyCoven rites for two, three and four witches, alone with AI sisters or with friends
